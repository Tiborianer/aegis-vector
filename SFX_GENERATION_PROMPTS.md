# Aegis Vector weapon sound generation briefs

The current game implements distinct layered Web Audio versions of these sounds. Generated samples can replace the tonal layers later without changing combat events. Request isolated, dry, single effects with no music, ambience, voices, trailer braams, or long reverb.

- **ARC fire, 0.16s:** heavy electromagnetic aircraft-cannon pulse; dense lower-mid impact, tight capacitor snap, short metallic pressure wave, futuristic but ballistic, no laser pew.
- **ARC impact, 0.18s:** hard energized metal strike with hot sparks and a compact electrical crack.
- **NOVA launch, 0.45s:** compact fighter missile launch; mechanical release thunk, powerful ignition bark, short turbulent rocket tail, no explosion.
- **NOVA impact, 0.55s:** compact high-energy detonation with forceful bass body, sharp debris crack, restrained plasma tail.
- **LANCE fire, 0.30s:** high-power beam discharge; immediate glassy transient, muscular energy body, clean cutting resonance, short sizzling tail, no retro ray-gun tone.
- **LANCE impact, 0.28s:** armor-cutting energy strike with white-hot hiss, stressed metal snap, very short molten tail.
- **WING fire, 0.12s:** light but punchy drone-cannon burst; precise mechanical tick and compressed ion pop, designed for fast repeated volleys and clearly lighter than ARC.
- **WING impact, 0.12s:** small energized spark burst with a tight mechanical ricochet.
- **ION fire, 0.42s:** violent controlled chain-lightning discharge; branching electrical cracks, resonant arc snap, rapidly connected micro-transients, no natural thunder.
- **ION impact, 0.30s:** concentrated electrical rupture with branching crackles and a brief high-voltage decay.

Preferred delivery is mono WAV at 48kHz/24-bit. Remove leading silence but keep the full initial transient.
